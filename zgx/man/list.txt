^Syntax:
^zg -list archive [--verbose] [--silent]

Lists the files in an archive without extracting them or modifying the archive.

[Display:
]Without --verbose: file names, preceded by the archive path.
]--verbose / --v: archive details and a table with date, sizes, compression ratio and stored CRC-32.
]--silent / --s: hides the banner; the list remains visible.

Values come from metadata: -list does not verify the integrity of file data.
To verify the archive, use: zg -test archive

The ZipBomb check can report a risk, but does not block a readable file list.
Warnings go to stderr; results go to stdout.
Risk codes: 41 warning, 42 high risk, 43 incomplete inspection.
For other codes: zg -help -exitcodes

[Examples:
*zg -list "C:\Archives\Documents.zip"
*zg -list "C:\Archives\Documents.zip" --verbose
