^Syntax:
^zg -optim file-or-folder [file-or-folder ...] [R0|R1] [--force] [global options]

Optimizes LibreOffice/OpenOffice documents and legacy WinAmp/WMP skins by rebuilding their ZIP container with maximum ZIP compression.

[Supported files:
]ODT, ODS, ODP, ODG, OTG, OTP, ODM, ODF, ODB, OTS, OTT, OXT, WSZ, WMZ, MSKIN, WAL and DSFZ.

[Options:
]R0 scans folders only at the first level (default).
]R1 or --recursive scans folders and subfolders.
]--force optimizes a file even when it is already marked as optimized.

%For every optimized file, zg creates or updates a <file>.BAK backup before replacing the original. Encrypted containers are refused because optimization can otherwise lose data.

*zg -optim report.odt
*zg -optim "C:\Documents" R1
